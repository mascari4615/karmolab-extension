Read YouTube and Arca.live your way, and manage useful site tools in one place.

KarmoLab lets you adjust comment layouts, video grids, unwanted content, site appearance and volume. Open the extension icon to switch between YouTube, Arca.live and site tools in one settings popup.

YouTube
- Use compact comments with adjustable text size and line height.
- Choose 2 to 6 videos per row on Home.
- Filter videos, Shorts and comments by keyword, and manage blocked authors and channels.
- Automatically activate YouTube's Korean translation button when available. Comments you switch back to the original are preserved.
- See Shorts publication dates, today's Shorts count, repeat Shorts visits and Shorts viewing time.
- Export settings and block lists as JSON and merge them when restoring.

Arca.live: optional, enabled by you
- Choose normal blocking or stronger blocking that also hides popular posts.
- Block a post's original channel, including in search results and mixed-channel feeds.
- Undo a block immediately, or find and unblock an author in settings.
- Automatically block authors based on posts encountered in selected channels and their public recent post history. This does not search every historical post.
- Browse followed authors' posts, track read and scrap markers, and view local activity information.
- Refresh open Arca.live tabs after enabling or disabling the feature. Some Arca.live controls are currently in Korean.

Site tools
- Enable dark mode on individual sites.
- Remember site volume and amplify it up to 300%.
- Turn Base64-encoded HTTP/HTTPS web addresses into clickable links.

Things to know
Comment translation works only when YouTube provides a Korean translation button. Home-only hiding also attempts YouTube's Not interested feedback for the clicked video. Removing a local hide does not undo feedback already sent.

Volume amplification above 100% uses audio capture from the tab where you apply it. It does not capture microphone audio or video, record audio, or upload audio.

Settings, block lists and feature activity records stay in browser storage and are not sent to a separate collection server. Arca.live requests for public posts, profiles and signed-in scrap lists use your existing site session. Arca.live and site tools apply only where you explicitly enable the feature and allow site access.
